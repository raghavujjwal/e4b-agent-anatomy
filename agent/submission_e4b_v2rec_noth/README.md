One-factor arm on `../submission_e4b_v2rec` (the shipped configuration): the earlier thoughts are not sent back (`include_thoughts: false`) while the thinking switch stays ON (`thinking_budget: 4096`). Everything else, the prompt included, is identical.

Why this change: in lab session 1 (2026-11-08, mined tune pool) the thinking-off arm `th0` removed the thinking switch AND the thoughts in the history at once. Its traces show that the switch really was off in the prompt, yet the model still reasoned in 45% of its turns (v2rec 95%), `edit_file` failed 72% of the time against 56%, and 7 episodes hit the call cap against 2. So that arm cannot say what the history does by itself. In the corrected harness a positive budget keeps `enable_thinking` true whatever `include_thoughts` says (`agent/vendor/scorer_port.py`, `thinking_request_fields`), and `include_thoughts: false` strips the reasoning from the assistant messages of the request (`agent/vendor/runner.py`). This arm therefore keeps the reasoning of every turn and drops about a third of the prompt (thoughts were 31% of it in the control runs). v2rec compacted its history in 26 of 70 lab episodes (th0, which kept no thoughts, in 12). Compacted episodes resolved 4 of 59 (7%) against 20 of 151 (13%) over the three lab runs, but they are also the long ones (mean 15.2 against 6.8 calls), and 16 of the 24 resolved episodes needed 6 calls or fewer. So the expected gain is small; the arm tests whether the context pressure is a cause or only a sign of a long, failing episode.

Decision rule, written before any run (2026-11-08): `docs/EVAL_PROTOCOL.md` rule 16. One seed on the mined tune pool next to a same-session `v2rec` control; the arm is a candidate only if it resolves at least 4 tasks more than that control, and a pass buys two more seeds of the arm and of the control, not a claim (four arms against one control pass by chance in 20 to 44% of sessions).
Read next to it, as secondary numbers: episodes with a compaction, edit failure rate, completion tokens, empty patches (`mine/lab_report.py` prints them).
